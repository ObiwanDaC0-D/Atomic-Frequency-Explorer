Why does this matter?

Frequency is not time itself

An atom does not experience time faster because a transition has a higher frequency. These are differences in quantum energy.

Light connects the scales

Speed of light = frequency × wavelength. Higher frequency means shorter wavelength in a vacuum.

Why hydrogen?

Hydrogen is the most abundant element in the universe. Its 21-centimeter line is important in radio astronomy.

Why cesium?

Cesium-133 defines the SI second. Other atomic transitions can also serve as extremely stable references.
